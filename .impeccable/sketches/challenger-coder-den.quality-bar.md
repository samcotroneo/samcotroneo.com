# Quality bar — Cozy Bedroom Coder Den

## Native devices

- Desktop: full 1200×900 scene with monitor-as-hub, desk, lamp, window, poster, books, mug.
- Mobile: simplified 900×1200 portrait scene; larger CRT, stacked world windows, readable tappable targets.

## Frame & depth

- Vignetted wall gradient + fractal noise + radial corner shadow for atmosphere.
- Dimensional desk: wood-grain-filtered top/front/legs with cast shadow.
- CRT monitor: bezeled body, stand, screen scanlines, vignette.
- Warm lamp: shade gradient, beam cone, wall glow, dust motes.

## Lettering treatment

- Display/name: Chakra Petch, uppercase, tight tracking, warm copper color.
- Body/tagline: Chakra Petch or DM Sans uppercase tracking; secondary text tinted from the ambient palette, never gray.
- Code labels: JetBrains Mono uppercase.

## Ornament density

- Desktop: full room details (window, poster, books, mug).
- Mobile: reduced to window, lamp, desk, CRT, mug; no poster/books.

## Motion

- One authored entrance: lamp drift / CRT flicker / subtle float on scroll hint.
- Hovering a world window throws that world’s color across the room (room-glow background shift).
- Reduced-motion fallback disables continuous animation.
