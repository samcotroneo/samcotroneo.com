A game-UI concept sheet of FOUR portrait project-card container designs arranged in a strict uniform grid of 2 columns x 2 rows. Each design must EXACTLY fill its own 512x768 cell, edge to edge, with zero gaps, zero margins and zero overlap between cells. Flat front-on view, no perspective, no drop shadows outside the card.

Style: 16-bit pixel art, SNES/GBA era game UI craft, crisp chunky pixels, limited palette, subtle dithering, dark outline work. Cohesive with a cozy night-time coder den: deep near-black purple (#14111f) cabinet bodies, warm amber (#e9c46a) marquee glow, copper (#f4a261) and muted teal (#2a9d8f) accents, magenta/cyan neon sparingly. Theme: retro arcade cabinets.

Every card must have THREE empty flat areas for later text overlay, in this layout:
1. A marquee band near the top (flat glowing solid color, no text, no letters, no numbers).
2. A recessed CRT screen window in the upper middle (solid very dark, faint scanline texture allowed, subtle screen curvature shading) about half the card width's height.
3. A large flat lower control panel (solid dark color, minimal texture, maybe a single joystick or buttons pushed to the far edges) for description text.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no icons anywhere in the art.

Four distinct designs, one per cell, left to right, top to bottom:
1. Classic upright cabinet front, black-purple laminate, amber glowing marquee, chunky coin slot at the bottom edge.
2. Neon synthwave cabinet, deep purple with cyan and magenta edge light strips, chrome trim.
3. Woodgrain 1970s Atari-style cabinet, warm walnut sides, brass marquee clips, copper control deck.
4. Sleek teal-grid tron-like terminal cabinet, dark body with glowing teal circuit seams and amber marquee.

Uniform lighting across all four cells: soft warm light from top-left, gentle bottom-right shading. Background of each cell fully covered by the card art itself (the card IS the full cell content).
