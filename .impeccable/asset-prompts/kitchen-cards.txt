A game-UI concept sheet of FOUR portrait project-card container designs arranged in a strict uniform grid of 2 columns x 2 rows. Each design must EXACTLY fill its own 512x768 cell, edge to edge, with zero gaps, zero margins and zero overlap between cells. Flat front-on view, no perspective, no drop shadows outside the card.

Style: 16-bit pixel art, SNES/GBA era game UI craft, crisp chunky pixels, limited palette, subtle dithering, dark outline work. Cohesive with a cozy night-time coder den: deep near-black purple (#14111f) accents, warm copper/amber (#f4a261, #e9c46a) and muted teal (#2a9d8f) highlights. Theme: a cozy pixel kitchen — recipe cards, wood, copper pans, warm paper.

Every card must have THREE empty flat areas for later text overlay, in this layout:
1. A title band near the top (flat solid color, no text, no letters, no numbers).
2. A recessed dark window in the upper middle (solid very dark purple-brown, minimal texture, like a screen or photo frame) about half the card width's height.
3. A large flat lower panel (solid light paper color or solid dark panel, minimal texture) for description text.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no icons anywhere in the art.

Four distinct designs, one per cell, left to right, top to bottom:
1. Handwritten recipe index card pinned to a corkboard edge, cream lined paper, torn tape strips on top corners, copper pin.
2. Wooden cutting-board plaque with a clipped recipe card, carved wood border, small copper rivets, hanging hole with twine at top.
3. Stained vintage cookbook page with an ornate copper-ink border, coffee ring stain in one corner, warm parchment.
4. Enamel and copper kitchen wall plaque, cream enamel center with copper rim like a saucepan lid, chunky bolts.

Uniform lighting across all four cells: soft warm light from top-left, gentle bottom-right shading. Background of each cell fully covered by the card art itself (the card IS the full cell content).
