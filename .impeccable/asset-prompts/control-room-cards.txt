A game-UI concept sheet of FOUR portrait project-card container designs arranged in a strict uniform grid of 2 columns x 2 rows. Each design must EXACTLY fill its own 512x768 cell, edge to edge, with zero gaps, zero margins and zero overlap between cells. Flat front-on view, no perspective, no drop shadows outside the card.

Style: 16-bit pixel art, SNES/GBA era game UI craft, crisp chunky pixels, limited palette, subtle dithering, dark outline work. Cohesive with a cozy night-time coder den: deep near-black purple (#14111f) chassis, muted teal (#2a9d8f) status lights, warm amber (#e9c46a) and copper (#f4a261) accents. Theme: a retro computer control room — consoles, terminals, racks, floppy drives.

Every card must have THREE empty flat areas for later text overlay, in this layout:
1. A label band near the top (flat solid color like a printed equipment label, no text, no letters, no numbers).
2. A recessed screen window in the upper middle (solid very dark, faint green-teal phosphor glow at edges allowed) about half the card width's height.
3. A large flat lower panel (solid color, minimal texture, small LEDs or switches pushed to the far edges) for description text.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no icons anywhere in the art.

Four distinct designs, one per cell, left to right, top to bottom:
1. Rack-mount server module, brushed dark metal, teal status LED column on the left edge, ventilation slots, thumbscrews.
2. Chunky retro desktop terminal console, warm grey-beige plastic, dark CRT window, keyboard ledge along the bottom.
3. 3.5 inch floppy disk drive bay panel, dark steel chassis with a big floppy slot under the screen window, amber activity LED.
4. Industrial control console slab, charcoal panel with chunky toggle switches and small round gauges pushed to the edges, copper nameplate strip.

Uniform lighting across all four cells: soft warm light from top-left, gentle bottom-right shading. Background of each cell fully covered by the card art itself (the card IS the full cell content).
